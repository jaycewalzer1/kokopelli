# Port Averill: the life of Nell Varga

A harbor town on the Michigan shore of Lake Huron, 1900 onward. Real calendar and climate, invented town, people, and ships. The lake is the world's one large fact: it sets the work, the weather, the money, and who comes home.

**Life clock:** 1941-04-09T04:12 to 2023-10-02T06:40, 43,381,588 minutes.

Nell Varga was born during breakup on Lake Huron and wanted to run her father's ferry. Polio and the rules of the time closed that door. Her brother's ship went down when she was sixteen, and no one could say where, because no one had properly charted that water. So she learned mathematics, talked her way onto a survey launch, raised two children between field seasons, came home to nurse her mother, and spent thirty-nine years making the chart that finally found him. She lived long enough to teach a town's children to read it.
