## Switches added (all off by default)

| Switch | Where | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `--fixes memory` | `city/agents/lunch_fixes.py`, `lunch_text.py` | prompt keeps "The conversation so far" (both voices, last 10 lines); diary notes that restate one of the last three are dropped |
| `--fixes repeats` | `lunch_wm.Lunch.check_repeat` | a line ≥ 0.75 similar (difflib on words) to one of the speaker's last 4 or the other's last 2 goes back to Qwen as a bad reply |
| `--fixes loops` | `lunch_wm.Lunch.to_sim`, `check` | approaching someone already within 3 m is refused with "You're already right next to X"; picking up a held or out-of-reach bowl is refused |
| `--decide-on-change` | `lunch_wm.Lunch.wants_to_decide` | an idle mind with nothing pending decides only if its painted frame changed (mean abs grey diff > 30) or after 3 quiet steps |
| `--view-prompts` | `lunch_text.view_prompt`, `prompts_for` | one world-model prompt per view for Fern Court; each person worded identically in both views |
| `--life DB` | `city/agents/lunch_life.py` | each person's generated past (life generator, year level) added under the persona |
| `KOKO_FEEL_EVENTS=1` | `mind/feeling.py` `known_event` | a view change or failed action counts as "something changed", so the feeling is appraised instead of carried |

`run.json` now records the switches (`switches`) and the per-stream prompts (`wm_prompts`).

## Tools

- `city/agents/lunch_bench.py`: `score <run dirs>` measures any lunch run; `run --brain parrot|scripted|qwen` runs the
  real `Lunch` loop on the test suite's `FakeWorld` (NavMap coordinates, 1.6 m/s, 10 s steps, the two bowls, Ana the
  barista) with the fake world model. With `--brain qwen`, Qwen3-VL-8B-Instruct-4bit is seeded per call and shown the
  owner's recorded painted frames (`out/bodies/demo/runs/wm/frames/<agent>/`, n-th decision → n-th frame) instead of
  the fake world model's test pattern. **This is a behavior bench, not a world-model test.**
- Metrics: `repeat_share` (lines ≥ 0.75 similar to the speaker's own last 4, or echoing the other's last 2),
  `longest_loop` (longest run of the same non-speech action by one mind), `reasked_answered_news` (a question about the
  other's news after the other already told it: Mara about bees/hive/swarm, Iris about banners/fair), `qwen_calls`
  and `calls_per_sim_minute` (decisions + rejected replies), routine milestones (first arrival, first pick-up).
- `ParrotBrain`: a fake brain built from the failure modes seen in the real logs (repeats its line, echoes, re-approaches,
  grabs bowl 1).

### Feeling system (mind/)

- Reproducing case: none of the recorded mind runs (`out/mind/qwen_8`, `qwen_90`, `qwen_change_20`, `mock_60`) has a
  single event tick: the mock sim keeps everything in view and no action fails (0 "It didn't work", 0 "Now in view").
  `qwen_90` reported "curious 0.3" on 90/90 ticks; `qwen_change_20` carried the feeling unasked on 19/19 ticks because
  Qwen's frame-only change check said "nothing changed". So feeling-vs-event agreement **cannot be measured on any
  existing run**: there are no events to agree with.
- Fix (behind `KOKO_FEEL_EVENTS=1`): the loop's own known events (view change, failed action) count as a change.
  Fake-brain measure (`tests/test_mind.py` `KnownEventTest`, inner change check always "no"): event tick appraised
  0/1 off → 1/1 on, with "What just changed: Now in view: ana. What you just tried didn't work." in the appraisal prompt.
- Not measured with real Qwen: it needs a scene where something happens. Earlier evidence (mind/MEASUREMENTS.md):
  once told what changed, Qwen moved the feeling on 11 of 16 appraisals.
- The lunch agents have no feeling system; I did not add one (that would be a feature).

## Item 5: Unreal changes for the owner (not made; I must not touch mac_server/)

1. **Ease head turns.** `mac_server/Source/SimWorld/Private/SWCharacterBase.cpp:971-974` turns the actor linearly
   (`Alpha = RotationElapsed / RotationDuration`), and the eye camera rides the actor's yaw
   (`SWHumanoid.cpp:80-92` sets only the camera's location), so every look_at/TurnAround is a constant-speed pan with
   hard starts and stops. Change: `const double Eased = FMath::InterpEaseInOut(0.0, 1.0, Alpha, 2.0);` and use `Eased`
   in `SetActorRotation`. Optional: a minimum turn time proportional to angle (e.g. 0.4 s + 0.6 s per 90°) in
   `StartRotation` (`SWCharacterBase.cpp:174-180`, `RotationTimeFraction` at `SWCharacterBase.h:168`).
2. **Keep head sway.** The camera location already follows the eye socket (it bobs with the head bone), but its
   rotation is locked to the actor's yaw. In `SWHumanoid.cpp` next to `Camera->SetRelativeLocation(LastAutoCameraRel)`,
   add a damped share of the head bone's rotation relative to the actor: read
   `GetMesh()->GetSocketRotation(EyeSocket)`, take pitch and roll relative to the actor, scale by ~0.3, clamp to ±4°,
   low-pass over ~0.2 s, and `Camera->SetRelativeRotation(...)`. Yaw stays the actor's so the agent's look_at stays exact.
   Needs a check that `vset /camera/N/rotation` still overrides it (same rule as `bCameraOverridden`).
3. **Replace the grey tank tops.** Every body has `"outfit": null` (`city/bodies/looks.json`, so `Bodies.json` too):
   the 2026-11-08 Core Data build's `WI_DefaultGarment` cut the torso (city/bodies/LOG.md, 17:1x), so outfits were
   removed and the default grey garment shows. The server already reads `outfit` (`SWBodies.cpp:218`) and attaches it
   leader-posed with seg ids. What is missing is assets: in the scratch project, `make_bodies.py`'s wardrobe step with a
   garment that doesn't use hidden-face removal (or with it turned off), then `write_parts.py` → `Bodies.json` `outfit`
   per person, `migrate_bodies.sh`, `package_bodies.sh`. Colours to match the prompts: Mara rust-red linen shirt and
   cream trousers; Iris tan canvas jacket, charcoal t-shirt, olive trousers; Ana sage apron over a white t-shirt
   (`lunch_text.LOOKS`). That is the bodies area's work (port 9004).
