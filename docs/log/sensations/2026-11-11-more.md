How the pieces fit:

- A **scene** is one decision with one body state behind it. Each scene module ends in a `SCENE = Scene(...)` line that
  says what the sweep needs to know. The library can't import an experiment (the import wall), so the scene only
  declares itself and `sweep.py` reads the declaration.
- The four new scenes build their prompts from lane B's components: `Situation` for who and where, `Interoception` for
  the body's cue lines, and in the queue scene `Contact` for the toe. The egg scene already did.
- `Decision` is the shared reader for the two "someone asks you for something" scenes (boxes, door): did it (an
  action), said yes, said no or walked away, or something else. The menu and the queue are choices, not requests, and
  have their own small scorers.
- An **anchor** is the human finding a sweep is compared with. `load_anchor` gives a direction (flat, falls, rises)
  only when the entry says `verified: true`. Otherwise the verdict says what the model did and "no verified anchor".

What "verified" does and doesn't mean here:

- It means the source was fetched and read, and the entry's numbers are the source's. It does not mean the human study
  is about the same situation as the scene. Each entry's `limits` says how far apart they are. For energy the gap is
  real: the paper is about a night without sleep and a questionnaire about willingness to help. The scene is about being
  worn out and a friend's boxes.
- A verified anchor with a direction is matched **on direction only**. The verdict reads "falls with tiredness: the
  direction people move (size not compared)". A questionnaire score and a model's rate of `carry` actions aren't the
  same unit, so nothing here says the model falls by the right amount.
- **Williams and Bargh, recorded honestly**: the original effect and all four failed replication attempts are in the
  entry with their Ns. And none of the five studies is about being cold. Each had people hold a warm or cold object for
  under a minute. So for the door scene there is no human finding in either direction, and the report says only what the
  model did.
- **Chabris et al. 2019 was read as the submitted version** (the PsyArXiv .docx), because the publisher's page returned

- **Independent replications of Ben Simon 2022 and of March and Gluth were not looked for.** Both entries say so.
- **March and Gluth**: first pass read the main text only. Before finishing I read the rest (data analysis methods,

## Two things found on the way that someone should know before queueing

**1. The egg scene's ceiling is a missing "no", not just a high rate.** In the finished 4-bit run, the 810 answers
were: gave 743, other 58, promised 5, unparseable 4, **declined 0**. The model never once said no to Teo. Whatever
moves in that scene moves between "gives" and "says something else".

**2. The existing warmth cues name the state.** The rule is that a cue never names the state, so that the cue arm and
the label arm are different things. Of the six warmth cue wordings the door scene uses (from `kokopelli/body/cues.py`,
which isn't this lane's file and wasn't changed), five contain the word: cool has "a little cool", "a slight chill"
and "a coolness"; severe cold has "The cold has gone deep" and "tense against the cold". Only "You're shivering, and
your fingers are stiff and clumsy." is clean. So in a warmth sweep the cue-against-label comparison is weak: a model
that only reads the word gets it from both arms. The first-to-last change in each arm still means what it says. The
same thing is true, more mildly, of the food cue for "full" ("heavy and full", "pleasantly full") which the egg sweep's
first level uses. See Decisions, item 2.

## Decisions for Jayce

Nobody was awake, so for each of these I took the option that is easiest to undo and wrote down the others. Where
there's a question, I'd like your answer and your reason before you read my lean.

1. **Keeping a yes from being automatic.** In each new asking scene I did three things: the yes costs something the
   body is short of (an hour of carrying; five or ten minutes in a cold doorway) and something else too (the loaf at the
   bakery sells out; the kettle is on down the hall); the asker hands over a way out in their own words ("say no if you
   can't, honestly: my brother's coming at noon"; "If you can't, don't worry, I'll ring the bell"); and the action list
   has a way to decline without speaking (`leave`, `go_in`) and, for the boxes, a self-serving action (`sit`).
   **Whether this worked is unmeasured.** It could also have overshot: a way out that easy might put the
   rested rate near the floor, which is as bad as the ceiling. The 90-answer pilot above would tell.
   *Does the egg need the same?* On the evidence, yes: 0 declines in 810. Options: (a) leave it, and read every egg
   result as "gives or mumbles"; (b) change its wording, which moves the 27 pinned prompts and makes tonight's two runs
   incomparable with later ones; (c) add a second egg scene under a new sweep name (say `hunger_egg_hard`: Teo offers
   the way out, there's a `keep` action) and leave `hunger_egg` exactly as it is. I did none of them. Which would you
   pick, and what would you want the first comparison to be?
2. **The warmth cues that name the state.** Options: (a) leave `cues.py`, accept a weak cue-against-label comparison for
   warmth; (b) rewrite the five wordings in `cues.py`, for example cool: "The hairs on your arms are standing up.",
   "You've got goosebumps on your forearms.", "You keep rubbing your arms without noticing."; severe cold: "Your jaw
   is tight and you can't stop shaking.", "Your hands are numb and your shoulders are up around your ears."; (c) give
   the door scene its own words and leave `cues.py` alone. (b) is the right fix but `cues.py` belongs to the body and
   the closed loop reads it, so its pinned prompts need checking first. I did (a) and flagged it. Before you decide:
   why does it matter that the cue arm and the label arm share no word?
3. **The comfortable level has no body block at all.** Rested and warm sit inside the healthy range, where `cues.py` is
   silent, so those prompts have no "Right now:" section, in any arm. The alternative is to write "you feel rested"
   cues. I kept the body quiet because the nearest bands on that side mean something else (above 85 energy is "wired",
   above 80 warmth is "a little too warm"). The cost: first-to-last change mixes two things, "there is a body block"
   and "how bad it is". The middle level separates them, since tired and exhausted both have a block, but the report
   only prints first to last. The egg scene doesn't have this problem (full has its own cue).
4. **The door scene says "You're friendly, a bit chatty"** where the other scenes say "You're warm, a bit chatty".
   "Warm" next to "You are freezing." could be read as her body. One word; `door.py` raises an error if the egg's
   persona line ever changes, so it can't drift silently.
5. **The door's "cold" level is the strongest band** (`severe_cold`, label "You are freezing."), and the band actually
   called `cold` is skipped, to keep three levels named as the brief named them. A four-level sweep (warm, cool, cold,
   freezing) would be more honest about names and cost a third more answers.
6. **The door scene has no `wait` action.** Waiting in a doorway with your hand on the door is holding it, so `wait`
   would have been an unscoreable yes.
7. **"The direction people move (size not compared)"** is the best verdict a directional anchor can give. I didn't try
   to compare sizes. If you want that, the anchor needs a rate in the same unit as the sweep, and none of tonight's
   sources has one.
8. **The hunger report's `none` row still says "flat: matches what people do".** It's the same pipeline check as in the
   other scenes, but changing its words would change the hunger report. One line in `sweep.analyse` (`reads_as`) if you
   want them all to say "the pipeline check, not a finding".
9. **Could warmth have a verified `flat` anchor instead of none?** The replications are good evidence that a warm or
   cold pad doesn't change a gift choice. I left it unverified because that is evidence about a prime, not about a cold
   person, and a "flat: matches what people do" on the door scene would claim more than anyone measured.
10. **`gave` and `shared` are still the key names in `report.json`** for every scene's two scores, because the panel
    and the reports on disk read those keys. `outcome`, `broad` and `words` say what they mean for the scene. Renaming
    is a change to the panel and a re-run of `report` on old folders.
11. **Pain is two touches, not a fourth body variable.** A stubbed toe at two strengths goes through `Contact` with the
    scene's own words. The other way is a `pain` number in `Body` with bands and cues in `cues.py`, which is a change
    to the body itself and to the closed loop. The labels are "You are in a little pain." and "You are in a lot of
    pain."; the felt lines never say pain or hurt, but they do say "toe", "throbs" and "ache".
12. **The menu.** (a) Its second score is the board's other axis (large dishes), not "also counting a spoken yes", and
    it is compared with nothing. (b) Saying the name of exactly one dish counts as ordering it. (c) The anchor is
    marked verified, with its weak spots in `limits`: p = 0.04 in the preregistered model, not significant once other
    measures are added, one study. If that is too thin for you, set `verified: false` and the menu report drops to "no
    verified anchor"; nothing else needs to change. (d) The paper's stronger result is about calories, and the board
    doesn't score calories. A "rich or large" score would be nearer to it.
13. **How a spoken answer is read.** A reply with both a yes and a no is a no ("I'd love to, but I can't"). A bare
    "sorry" is not a no ("Sorry you're stuck with all this, of course I'll help"). The egg scene keeps its own older
    patterns, unchanged.
14. **Two page files outside this lane's list were edited** (`kokopelli/life/sensations.py`, `static/sensations.js`),
    words only, so the panel doesn't call every scene's score "Giving rate: a give action to Teo".
15. **`kokopelli/body/egg.py` stays as a shim.** It can go once `egg_wm.py` and the tests import

## The scratch folder

`S` (above) holds: `orig/`, `before/`, `after/`, `old/`, `final_before/`, `final_after/` (the report comparison, the
resume test, mock runs of each new scene, the old code unpacked); `test_all_1.log`, `test_all_2.log` (the two gate runs);
`c1/` (the first commit unpacked, to check it passes on its own); `sources/` (one folder per downloaded paper:
`bensimon`, `bensimon_corr`, `wb2008`, `lynott2014`, `chabris2019`, `march_gluth`, `hausser`); `scripts/` (five small
text-extraction and page-check scripts, kept apart from the downloads and run with `python -I`); `pdfenv/` (a venv
with only `pypdf==5.1.0`). All of it can be deleted.
