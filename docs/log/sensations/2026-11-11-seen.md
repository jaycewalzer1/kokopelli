## How each backend runs (the shared `.venv` has no torch)

Nothing was installed anywhere and no new venv was made. `simworld/.venv` (Python 3.10) already has `torch` 2.14.1 with
Metal and `transformers` 5.20.2, which includes OWLv2, Grounding DINO and Florence-2 natively (no remote code). So
`seen.py` starts a small worker under that interpreter (`python -m kokopelli.wm.seen --worker owlv2`) and talks to it
over a pipe, one JSON line per frame. `seen.py` is therefore written to import on Python 3.10 and is its own worker.
The worker loads weights from the Hugging Face cache at a pinned revision with the network off.

The MLX route (Florence-2 through `mlx-vlm` in the shared `.venv`, no worker at all) was **not built or measured**:
only the fine-tuned "base-ft" checkpoint exists in MLX form, converted with mlx-vlm 0.1.0 (the venv has 0.7.11), and it
could not be tried outside the queue. Its weights are downloaded (`mlx-community/Florence-2-base-ft-bf16`) and unused.

**How the truth was built** (all in `seen_eval.py`, CPU only): a frame has a person if the seg control has a connected
region of at least 150 pixels in one of three palette colours. I found those colours by hand: cyan `(26,229,231)` and
olive `(132,151,55)` are the other agent's body in shade and in sun in Mara's view, yellow `(231,228,72)` and cyan are
Mara's body in Iris's view (the control's colour is the sim's actor crossed with a material class guessed from the
render's colour, so one body changes colour with the light). "Nobody" is strict: no patch of those colours (8 pixels;
12 for olive, which also appears as codec specks), and no person-sized region within 5 s either side, because a
far-off walker is a few smeared pixels before she is big enough to box. Everything else is "unclear" and left out.
I checked the labels by eye on the audit sheets, at thumbnail size: every "nobody" frame with the sim render beside the painting (no body in any sim render), and a sample of the boxes.

Frames: every 0.5 s up to 52 s (where the run's README says the films turn to mosaic), every 1 s after. Headline part:
210 frames, **122 scored (72 with a person, 50 with nobody)**, 88 unclear. Fewer than the plan's 200; sampling the
same 52 s four times denser would reach 200 with near-duplicate frames, so I didn't. Late part: 136 frames, all with a
person (the two stand facing each other), scored apart.

**What a false positive means here.** Cosmos paints passers-by of its own (its prompt mentions neighbours): in Mara's
first 10 s the sim shows an empty courtyard and the painting has people walking in it. A detector that finds them is
right about the picture and wrong about the candidate. With no box on the candidate, seen() can't tell the difference,
and the score counts it as the leak surviving.
