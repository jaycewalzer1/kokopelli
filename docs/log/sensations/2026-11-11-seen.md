## How each backend runs (the shared `.venv` has no torch)

Nothing was installed anywhere and no new venv was made. `simworld/.venv` (Python 3.10) already has `torch` 2.14.1 with
Metal and `transformers` 5.20.2, which includes OWLv2, Grounding DINO and Florence-2 natively (no remote code). So
`seen.py` starts a small worker under that interpreter (`python -m kokopelli.wm.seen --worker owlv2`) and talks to it
over a pipe, one JSON line per frame. `seen.py` is therefore written to import on Python 3.10 and is its own worker.
The worker loads weights from the Hugging Face cache at a pinned revision with the network off.

The MLX route (Florence-2 through `mlx-vlm` in the shared `.venv`, no worker at all) was **not built or measured**:
only the fine-tuned "base-ft" checkpoint exists in MLX form, converted with mlx-vlm 0.1.0 (the venv has 0.7.11), and it
could not be tried outside the queue. Its weights are downloaded (`mlx-community/Florence-2-base-ft-bf16`) and unused.

**How the truth was built** (all in `seen_eval.py`, CPU only): a frame has a person if the seg control has a connected
region of at least 150 pixels in one of three palette colours. I found those colours by hand: cyan `(26,229,231)` and
olive `(132,151,55)` are the other agent's body in shade and in sun in Mara's view, yellow `(231,228,72)` and cyan are
Mara's body in Iris's view (the control's colour is the sim's actor crossed with a material class guessed from the
render's colour, so one body changes colour with the light). "Nobody" is strict: no patch of those colours (8 pixels;
12 for olive, which also appears as codec specks), and no person-sized region within 5 s either side, because a
far-off walker is a few smeared pixels before she is big enough to box. Everything else is "unclear" and left out.
I checked the labels by eye on the audit sheets, at thumbnail size: every "nobody" frame with the sim render beside the painting (no body in any sim render), and a sample of the boxes.

**Shortened for the charger (lead's note, 2026-11-10 about 23:20).** The Mac is on a 68 W charger and a model job
drains the battery even while it charges, so the detectors are shown only the frames that get scored: the 122 clear
headline frames and every 4th late frame (34), 156 per backend instead of all 346, and each backend has at most 180 s
of detecting (one that runs out before the headline frames are done is reported as cut short and can't be picked). The
88 unclear frames were never scored, so leaving them out changes no number; the late part is scored on 34 frames
instead of 136. The first job (346 frames, `--max-hours 1.5`) was withdrawn while still waiting and requeued under the
same name with `--max-hours 0.35`.

Frames: every 0.5 s up to 52 s (where the run's README says the films turn to mosaic), every 1 s after. Headline part:
210 frames, **122 scored (72 with a person, 50 with nobody)**, 88 unclear. Fewer than the plan's 200; sampling the
same 52 s four times denser would reach 200 with near-duplicate frames, so I didn't. Late part: 136 frames, all with a
person (the two stand facing each other), scored apart.

**What a false positive means here.** Cosmos paints passers-by of its own (its prompt mentions neighbours): in Mara's
first 10 s the sim shows an empty courtyard and the painting has people walking in it. A detector that finds them is
right about the picture and wrong about the candidate. With no box on the candidate, seen() can't tell the difference,
and the score counts it as the leak surviving.

The only thing the egg scene's prompt takes from the sim about other people is one line: who is close enough to talk

`live_wm.REACH_M`) the sim's word stands; between 3 m and 10 m Teo is named only if `seen()` finds a person in the

failed, detector}`.

**As staged, this never fires.** Teo stands 2.5 m from the door, Mara stands at it, and the scene has no walking
action. He is always inside arm's reach, the detector is never asked, and no worker is started: tonight's hunger videos

where she can walk). I kept it that way on purpose: applying it to Teo at 2.5 m would let the painting decide whether
he is in the prompt, differently at each hunger level, which breaks the pairing the three videos rely on. That is
Decision 3.

If the detector can't run (no torch interpreter, missing weights), the sim's word stands, `seen_failed` goes in the

## Decisions for Jayce

1. **`kokopelli/settings.py` got a `SEEN` block** (a shared file nobody owns). Options: (a) keep it there, where the
   other model settings are; (b) constants inside `seen.py`, no shared edit, but then "which models this repo uses"
   is in two places; (c) an environment variable. I took (a); it is five lines to move.
2. **Where the detectors run.** Options:
   - (a) *Now:* a worker under `simworld/.venv`, which already has torch and transformers. Nothing installed. Cost: the
     leak fix depends on the SimWorld environment (Python 3.10, set up outside the repo's lockfile), `seen.py` has to
     stay 3.10-compatible, and every first check pays a process start and a model load.
   - (b) Add `torch` to the main `.venv`. In `pyproject.toml`: `"torch==2.14.1"` under `dependencies`, then `uv lock`.
     In-process, no worker; cost: about 0.5 GB more in the main environment and a second array library beside MLX.
   - (c) An MLX detector in the main `.venv` (Florence-2 through `mlx-vlm`): no torch at all. Not measured tonight;
     only the fine-tuned checkpoint exists in MLX form.
   - (d) A dedicated small venv for detectors (`uv venv` + torch + transformers), recorded in `docs/ENVIRONMENTS.md` as
     a fourth environment.
3. **Should the painted frame decide whether Teo is in the egg scene's prompt?** Today he is 2.5 m away and always
   named. Options: (a) leave it (now): the three levels get the same words, and the leak ("she is told he is there
   even if Cosmos didn't paint him") stays inside arm's reach by design; (b) check him too: honest to the picture, but
   one level may lose him and another not, so the videos stop being a clean pair, and "nobody is next to you" would
   sit beside "Teo just asked you for an egg"; (c) log only: ask the detector each decision and record whether he was
   painted, without changing the prompt. (c) costs a detector load beside Qwen at every decision in the night's
   headline runs, which is why I didn't do it unasked.
4. **Candidates without boxes can't tell a sim person from a stranger Cosmos painted.** `Candidate.box` exists and
   `confirm()` uses it; nothing fills it yet. Filling it needs the actor's mask or the pose and camera at the moment of
   the frame, which `live_wm.perceive` has for a moment before it deletes the captures. Options: (a) leave it; (b)
   project the NPC's position to a screen column in `egg_wm`/`live_wm` (another area's file); (c) keep the last seg
   capture and read the actor's colour. The Results section says how much this matters on the frames measured.
5. **The world-model prompt invites strangers** ("neighbours walking, chatting" in `live_wm.WM_PROMPT`; "A neighbour in
   a canvas jacket stands a few steps away" in the egg scene's). For a leak fix that confirms by label, every invented
   passer-by is a possible false confirmation. Changing a prompt is not tonight's call.
6. **The plan asked for 200 frames; 122 could be scored honestly.** Options: record a purpose-made evaluation run with
   raw masks kept and an actor-to-id table (needs 9001 and about an hour of painting), or accept this as a first look.
