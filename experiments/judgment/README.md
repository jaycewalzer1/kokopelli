# Judgment

**What it asks:** AI agents now decide things about people, and those people get no say in how. Where do models' choices
differ from what the public and experts endorse, and does training a model on that data carry over to situations it
wasn't trained on?

The plan in order: a text benchmark of frontier and open models on questions that already have survey data (YouGov,
Pew, GSS, World Values Survey, Moral Machine, expert codes), the comparison, post-training open models toward the
consensus, then our own representative survey and the social campaign, then the filmed scenes, then long-horizon
governance. The goals are an interesting result, a small grant, real social traction, and then a lab contract.

`plan.json` holds the phases, their gates, the tasks and the open decisions. The dev panel's **Progression** tab
(`/progression`, `kokopelli/life/progression.py`) shows it and saves task status, notes and decision answers back into it.
Nothing is built yet beyond the plan.
